RangeKitby WorkinWithAI

Does your song feel flat, strained or mumbly when you sing it? It's usually the key, not your voice. Sing your lowest and highest notes, sing or play the melody, and RangeKit scores all 12 keys against your range, shows why, and plays the band in the key that fits you, so you can A/B it before you record.

iPhone/iPad: flip the silent switch off (or use headphones). iOS mutes web audio when the ringer is silenced. Exports open the share sheet: pick "Save to Files".
